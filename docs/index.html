Scope · ICSE

The International Conference on Software Engineering

Where TopicDrift began: ~11k ICSE papers (1976–2025), each assigned to the same global topics used by every tab. The streamgraph traces how broad research themes rose and fell; the treemap breaks each decade down into its themes and topics.

Research Theme Drift

Overarching themes' share of ICSE papers over 5-year periods. Toggle Share % / Paper Count; click any band for its sub-topics and each one's share of that theme.

Corpus Composition

Decade → topic. Tile area is paper volume; colour is the topic's identity. Toggle to recolour by citation impact, growth vs. the previous decade, or newly-emerged topics — and click any tile for a decade's era summary or a topic's full stats and papers.

Topic Drift Search

Type a keyword — mobile, llm, testing — or click a historical milestone chip to find matching ICSE topics. Selected topics overlay their drift curves, with a star at each topic's peak period; the cards list each topic's most-cited papers.

Scope · Top 10

Ten flagship software-engineering & PL venues

ICSE, ESEC/FSE, ASE, ISSTA, ICSME, MSR, RE, OOPSLA, PLDI and POPL — the field's most selective conferences, sharing the same topic space as the other tabs so the themes are directly comparable.

Scope · All Conferences

Every venue with usable abstracts
