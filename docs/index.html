About

What is TopicDrift?

TopicDrift is a data-driven study of how research priorities shift over time at long-running software-engineering conferences. We combine DBLP metadata with OpenAlex abstracts and citation counts to apply BERTopic clustering across ~7,800 ICSE papers, then track how each topic's share of the field grows, peaks, and fades across decades.
