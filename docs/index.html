About

What is TopicDrift?

TopicDrift is a data-driven study of how research priorities shift over time at long-running software-engineering conferences. We combine DBLP metadata with OpenAlex abstracts and citation counts to apply BERTopic clustering across ~7,800 ICSE papers, then track how each topic's share of the field grows, peaks, and fades across decades.

Topic Treemap

A hierarchical breakdown of the ICSE corpus by decade and topic. Tile area reflects paper volume; switch color modes — topic identity, citation impact, growth vs. the previous decade, or emerging topics. Hover for stats and click any tile for its description and papers.

Topic Drift Search

Type a keyword — mobile, llm, testing — or click one of the historical milestone chips to find the topics that match. Selected topics overlay their drift curves on a single axis, with a star marking each topic's peak 5-year bucket. The cards below list each topic's most-cited papers.
