Scope · Full corpus

Every venue with usable abstracts

2,000+ DBLP conferences (≥50 papers and ≥50% abstract coverage) spanning all of computer science. The same global topics, now seen across the whole field — watch areas like AI and security swell over the decades.

Read this scope as directional, not authoritative.
  • Topic and theme accuracy is not yet where it should be at this scale — expect a meaningful share of coarse or mislabeled assignments.
  • The corpus currently pulls in more than computing-related papers, so non-CS work leaks in and distorts the themes.
  • More data cleaning and better topic/theme fitting are still needed before the full-corpus numbers can be trusted precisely. The ICSE scope is the most validated.
